# FocusGuard privacy policy

FocusGuard accesses Android app usage only while tracking is enabled. It uses foreground app events to count screen time. For apps you enable as Focus Breakers, it stores app names, package identifiers, and session times on the device. It also stores daily totals, block counts, and your preferences locally.

FocusGuard processes this data on the device and does not send it to the developer or use it for advertising. The Android app excludes stored usage and preferences from its backup and device-transfer configuration.

Use **Stop tracking** in the app or its tracking notification to stop collection. Use **Settings → Clear all my data** to pause tracking and delete recorded history and your Focus Breakers. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. FocusGuard keeps history until you clear it or uninstall the app.

Usage access allows tracking. Display over other apps lets FocusGuard show a blocking screen for enabled Focus Breakers. Notification permission controls reminders and daily summaries. You can revoke these permissions in Android settings.

For privacy inquiries, contact the FocusGuard developer through the repository's [issue tracker](https://github.com/Surya9918/FocusGuard2.0/issues). Avoid posting personal usage data in a public issue.
