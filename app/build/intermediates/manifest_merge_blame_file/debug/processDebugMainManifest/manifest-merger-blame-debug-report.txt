5    Permission Audit & Justification:
6    ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7
8    PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS
9      ↳ Special permission — user must grant via Settings > Digital Wellbeing
10        or Settings > Apps > Special App Access > Usage Access.
11      ↳ Required to read which app is currently active via UsageStatsManager.
12      ↳ Cannot be granted via runtime dialog. Must deep-link to settings screen.
13
14    SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (Display over other apps)
15      ↳ Special permission — user grants via Settings > Apps > Special Access.
16      ↳ Required for BlockScreenActivity to overlay on top of any app.
17      ↳ On Android 12+, this permission has been tightened. The overlay window
18        must use TYPE_APPLICATION_OVERLAY (not deprecated TYPE_PHONE/SYSTEM).
19
20    FOREGROUND_SERVICE + FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE
21      ↳ FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Allows the FocusTrackerService to run persistently
22        in the foreground with a persistent notification (Android 9+ requirement).
23      ↳ FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE: Required on Android 14+ for any foreground
24        service not covered by a specific type. Declared here as a fallback.
25      ↳ Note: We also declare FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC as the service performs
26        continuous data monitoring — this aligns with Play Store policy.
27
28    POST_NOTIFICATIONS
29      ↳ Runtime permission required on Android 13+ (API 33+).
30      ↳ Required to show the persistent "FocusGuard is active" notification
31        that all foreground services must display.
32      ↳ Requested at runtime during onboarding.
33
34    RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
35      ↳ Normal permission — auto-granted, no user interaction needed.
36      ↳ Allows FocusGuard to restart the tracking service after device reboot.
37      ↳ Critical for uninterrupted wellbeing tracking.
38
39    REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES
40      ↳ NOT included intentionally — FocusGuard never installs packages.
41        Including unnecessary permissions reduces user trust and Play Store risk.
42    ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
